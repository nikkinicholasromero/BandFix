How to run this test

First: turn BandFix's Correction off, leave brightness where you normally keep it, and press F for fullscreen.

On each page: look where the band crosses each row. Tick the label of every row where you can see the band.

Click a row to fill the screen with that colour. Use ↑ ↓ to step through levels, V to tick the level, and click again to go back.

Edge marks: M shows ticks at 864, 1080 and 1118 pt, to check which right edge is correct.

When finished: open Results and paste the text into the chat.
